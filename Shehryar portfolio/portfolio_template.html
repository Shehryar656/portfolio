About Me

Infrastructure that speaks for itself.

I'm a DevOps engineer who cares about the unglamorous parts of software: the pipelines that ship code, the infrastructure that runs it, and the alerts that — hopefully rarely — fire. My work sits between engineering and operations. I own cloud platforms end to end: architecture and infrastructure as code on one side, deployment strategy and incident response on the other.

Over the past year and a half of working remotely with international teams, I've led Kubernetes migrations, built self-service CI/CD factories, hardened cloud accounts against real-world threats, and cut both deploy times and cloud spend by double digits. I work the way I'd want a senior engineer to work: small reviewed changes, everything in version control, and an on-call mindset even on days I'm not on call.

When I'm not shipping infrastructure, I'm documenting it — because a pipeline nobody can understand is just a future incident with a longer lead time.

“If it isn't automated, it isn't done. If it isn't documented, it isn't ours.”
