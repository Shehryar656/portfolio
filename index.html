About Me

The person behind the infrastructure.

I'm a DevOps engineer who cares about the unglamorous parts of software: the pipelines that ship code, the infrastructure that runs it, and the alerts that — hopefully rarely — fire. I work with teams of 2 to 30 engineers who want a cloud they can stop worrying about.

In the past year and a half of working remotely with international teams, I've led Kubernetes migrations, built self-service CI/CD factories, hardened cloud accounts against real threats, and cut both deploy times and cloud spend by double digits. I work the way I'd want a senior engineer to work: small reviewed changes, everything in version control, and an on-call mindset even on days I'm not on call.

FAQ

Questions clients ask before starting.

How does it actually start?
You message me on WhatsApp with a few lines about your setup. We book a free 20-minute call, I audit your infrastructure, and you get a written report plus a fixed quote. If I think you don't need me, I'll say so — the report is yours either way.
Do you work remotely? Which time zones?
100% remote, worldwide. I overlap with GMT ±8 — that covers Europe, the Gulf, the UK and East Asia mornings. Everything else runs on async: written updates, Loom-style walkthroughs and a shared project board you can check anytime.
What happens if something breaks after you're done?
Three layers: (1) every project includes full documentation and a handover session, so your team owns it; (2) 30 days of included support at no extra cost; (3) if you want me on call long-term, that's the monthly care plan — pause anytime, no lock-in.
Do you sign NDAs?
Yes — before you share anything. Your architecture, your code, your bill: all confidential. I work in your accounts or fully isolated ones, never copy your code to my own machines, and hand over 100% of credentials and repos when we finish.
How do you charge?
Two models: fixed-price projects (scope, timeline and one number — agreed before work starts) and monthly care plans for ongoing work. No hourly billing, no surprise invoices. Payment milestones are agreed in the roadmap document.
My setup is honestly a mess. Can you even fix it?
That's literally the rescue service. The audit tells you, in plain English, what's fixable, what needs replacing, and what each option costs. Some “messes” are a 2-week job; some need a rebuild. You'll know before you pay for anything — and the report is yours to keep.
